V13.16 — SINGLE CATEGORY AUTO-ASSIGN

Change:
- When exactly one auction category exists, all approved non-captain auction players are automatically assigned to that category.
- Runs during Pre-Auction Setup data load, so it also handles players approved later and categories reduced to one.
- Captain registrations remain uncategorized and are excluded from the auction.
- When two or more categories exist, player category assignments are not changed.

Deployment:
- Replace admin-pre-auction.html.
- No Supabase SQL migration required.

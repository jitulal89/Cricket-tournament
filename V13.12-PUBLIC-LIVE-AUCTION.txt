V13.12 - PUBLIC LIVE AUCTION

Added public-auction.html:
- Public, no-login spectator page.
- Shows current live player profile and cricket statistics.
- Shows current bid, minimum bid and leading team.
- Shows teams and players sold with winning price.
- Subscribes to auction session and squad changes; also refreshes periodically as fallback.
- Read-only: no bidding or admin actions are exposed.

Added Share Live Auction button to Admin Live Auction page.
Added Watch Live Auction link to public Tournament page.
No database schema changes included.

Share URL format:
public-auction.html?slug=YOUR-TOURNAMENT-SLUG

The existing Supabase public read policies for auction_sessions, auction_squad, player data,
registrations, teams and categories are required for all spectator details to load.
